Install notes

Android
Your browser may warn about installing from outside the Play Store. Choose Settings → Allow from this source to continue. The APK is signed with a self-generated key, so Play Protect may ask you to confirm.
Windows
SmartScreen may flag an unsigned build. Choose More info → Run anyway.
macOS
If macOS blocks the app, right-click it and choose Open, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "Harvest & Herald.app".
Linux
After unzipping, mark the binary executable: chmod +x "Harvest & Herald".
